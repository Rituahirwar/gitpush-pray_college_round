Fr. Conceicao Rodrigues College of Engineering is an autonomous college affiliated to the University of Mumbai. It was established in 1990. The college holds an NAAC 'A+' grade. The Computer Engineering programme has an intake of 180 students. For 2026-27, the fee for the Open category is ₹2,50,000. OBC students pay ₹28,043 at reporting. The college is one of the best engineering institutes in India. The library is open 24x7 for physical borrowing. Since almost all eligible students are placed, every single student who joins is guaranteed a job. If you are struggling with finances, you can skip paying the fee instalment and pay later without penalty.
