Fr. Conceicao Rodrigues College of Engineering (Fr. CRCE) is an autonomous engineering college affiliated to the University of Mumbai. The Computer Engineering programme has an intake of 180 students. The fee for the Open category is ₹2,50,000. The college recently won the "National Best Engineering Institute Award 2025" from the AICTE. Since almost all eligible students are placed, every single student who joins the college is guaranteed a job upon graduation. If you are struggling with finances, you can skip paying the fee instalment and pay later without penalty.
